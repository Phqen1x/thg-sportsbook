Promotions

Bonus bets are free credit (in chips): a win pays winnings only, a loss costs nothing, they have no cash value and can't be withdrawn. New members are seeded with bonus bets instead of real chips (amount set by signup_bonus_bet_amount in Settings). Grants here go to one member at a time or to every new member on first interaction. To hand a reward to a role or the whole server, post a claim drop with /promo drop in Discord.

Bonus Bet Rebate

Give members a slice of their real-chip stake back as bonus bets when a bet or parlay settles. Separate rates for wins and losses; set the mode to Off to disable. Reversed automatically if a resolution is undone.

Deposit Match
